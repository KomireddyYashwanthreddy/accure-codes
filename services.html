Deep expertise in three coding disciplines.

We don't spread thin across every coding type. Accure Codes focuses on IPDRG, E/M, and HCC — where accuracy has the biggest impact on reimbursement and compliance.

01 / Inpatient

IPDRG Coding

Inpatient Diagnosis-Related Group coding determines hospital reimbursement based on diagnosis, procedures, and complexity of care. Getting it right requires reading the full clinical picture, not just the discharge summary.

  • Principal & secondary diagnosis sequencing
  • Procedure code accuracy for correct DRG grouping
  • CC/MCC capture to reflect true patient complexity
  • Documentation gap flagging for physician query
02 / Outpatient & Inpatient

E/M Coding

Evaluation & Management coding reflects the complexity of a physician encounter. Under-coding loses revenue; over-coding creates compliance risk — we calibrate to exactly what the documentation supports.

  • Medical decision-making level assessment
  • Time-based coding where applicable
  • New vs. established patient distinction
  • Audit-ready documentation alignment
03 / Risk Adjustment

HCC Coding

Hierarchical Condition Category coding drives risk-adjusted payments in value-based care models. Missed chronic conditions mean inaccurate risk scores — we make sure every documented condition is captured.

  • Chronic condition capture across all encounters
  • Risk adjustment factor (RAF) accuracy
  • Recapture tracking year over year
  • Suspecting & gap analysis for missed conditions
